# CSP-11 D703 Pre-live Qualification (DS-6)

This focused session is an evidence index for the DS-1-owned D703 decision. It
does not introduce independent design authority.

## Locked boundary

- D699/D696 is the exact six-arm baseline; D702 mutation-first recovery is the
  sole treatment delta.
- The matched runner may install only a binding-owned D702 continuation port
  sharing the existing budget, retry and actual-usage ledger.
- D703 owns distinct package-private observation, scorecard, generation and
  preflight identities. Historical D696/D699 bytes remain immutable.

  required before any live decision.
- `causalAttribution=undetermined` and `efficacyClaim=none` are fixed.
- No credential, control-plane, provider/network, charge or live replacement is
  authorized by D703.
