Concepts

The graph is the coordination surface.

GraphReFly organizes work as inspectable topology: nodes exchange wave messages, the dispatcher invokes functions, pools and bridges keep async or remote work outside the sync core, and packages expose language-native helpers over the same behavior.

GraphA single-thread concurrency domain with declared nodes and edges.
WaveOne message array moving through the graph as one ordered update.
BoundaryAsync sources, worker pools, and wire bridges re-enter through graph-visible facts.
